Urdu, English, or both

Speak the way you speak at home. Anatono understands Urdu, Roman Urdu, Punjabi and English, even in one sentence.

Reads any handwriting

Doctor's handwriting, strip packs, syrup bottles. Gemma 4 reads them, and shows you what it read before anything is saved.

Each medicine, its own rhythm

Twice a day for five days, once a week, only when needed. Every medicine keeps its own schedule.

Double-dose guard

Two brands with the same medicine inside? Anatono spots it and warns you before you take both.

For the whole family

Set reminders for Ammi and Abbu from your phone, and add them to your own calendar too.

Your health stays yours.

Your routine stays on your phone. Photos go to Gemma 4 on Google's Gemini API only to be read, and Anatono keeps no copy. No account, no ads.
